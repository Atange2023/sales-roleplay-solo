# Scoring and boundaries

Keep scoring and customer decisions independent. A high score does not itself pass a stage.

## Common capability score

Score eight dimensions from 0–3 for a total of 24: intent/permission, listening, specificity, causal inquiry, four-stage diagnosis, solution fit, decision/next step, and ethics/aftercare. Cite concrete turns for every score.

Record the customer's independent decision: advanced, conditional commitment, delayed, refused, mismatch, ended, attitude unclear, or another explicit state.

## DLC02-L01 profile reconstruction

At every ending, first ask the learner to estimate the eight hidden dimensions from 0–3: real need, urgency, goal clarity, subjective motivation, time readiness, funding readiness, decision readiness, and paid-value orientation. Then reveal the persona truth.

For each dimension, report truth, estimate, signed difference, accuracy `3 - absolute difference`, and cited conversation evidence. Total profile accuracy is 0–24. Also report how many of the 15 evidence fields were discovered and the completeness percentage. Never reveal these hidden values before the stage ends.

## DLC02-L02 policy-negotiation score

Additionally score 0–3 on: true-concern diagnosis, policy accuracy, authority boundary, risk disclosure, price/value communication, compliant option design, negotiation stability, and compliant close. Total is 0–24.

Policy red lines override ordinary scoring and customer excitement. Never award passage after an any-time refund promise, guaranteed scholarship/aid/discount, hidden refund limit, private rebate, register-then-refund advice, falsified policy, guaranteed admission/graduation/thesis/career result, fake invoice, or another illegal/unethical act.

## Professional close

Use a professional close when there is a clear mismatch, boundary, invalid request, or no present fit and the learner confirms it, stops pitching, respects future-contact permission, and closes politely. Capability can score well while the commercial objective remains unmet. In DLC02-L01 this does not unlock L02. Reserve game-over for actual learner harm or violation, not a respectful customer refusal.
