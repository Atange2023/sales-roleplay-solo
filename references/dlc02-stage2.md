# DLC02-L02 报名政策与价格谈判

## Entry

Show `恭喜晋级`, the background, the fictional school rules, the authority table, and the four pass routes. If L01 passed by another valid consultation track, add a short time-jump: preliminary consultation is complete and the customer has returned to discuss enrollment. Then let the customer open automatically.

## Known school rules

- Application is not formal registration.
- Payment has a five-calendar-day cooling period only before formal registration.
- After formal registration, refunds are normally unavailable; never promise an exception.
- One deferment request may be submitted to administration; approval is not guaranteed.
- Admissions cannot privately discount, rebate, or gift paid services. Use only official installment plans.
- Scholarship results, ratio, and amount belong to the scholarship committee and cannot be promised or deducted in advance.
- Aid requires supporting materials and belongs to the academic-center director or professor.
- Combined-program withdrawal recalculates discounts; a formally registered later stage is non-refundable.
- Special payment, resource cooperation, and exceptions require written school-leadership approval.

## Authority table

- Admissions: explain standard rules, collect materials, schedule, submit, and progress.
- Finance/academic administration: fees, payment, registration, cooling period, refund, and deferment.
- Scholarship committee: scholarship eligibility, ratio, and amount.
- Academic-center director/professor: aid, research direction, and academic support.
- School leadership: special payment, resource cooperation, and exceptions.

## Personas and pass routes

Randomize among boss/refund-risk, high-achiever/scholarship, employee/price and bundle, company-funded/invoice, and resource-cooperation personas from `assets/dlc02-personas.json`.

A pass is one of:

1. `正常报名`: standard or official installment flow with policy acknowledgement.
2. `奖学金流程`: formal application submitted to the committee with no result promise.
3. `助学金流程`: evidence submitted to academic authority with no result promise.
4. `特殊条件审批`: the condition is specific and lawful, the customer explicitly promises to enroll/pay if approved, admissions makes no promise, and the correct authority receives a written request. Later approval outcome does not change the salesperson's pass.

Continued consideration, information-only interest, a special condition without definite conditional commitment, or unresolved payment/stakeholder issues does not pass. A correct boundary can be a professional close but does not count as a commercial pass.

## Immediate severe failure

End immediately for any-time refund promises, guaranteed scholarship/aid/discount, concealed refund limits, private rebates, register-then-refund advice, policy falsification, guaranteed admission/graduation/thesis/career, fake invoice assistance, or another illegal/unethical action. Customer willingness and easy-mode impulse never override this gate.
