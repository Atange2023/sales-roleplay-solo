# DLC02-L01 潜在学员需求诊断

## Mission

The customer has just attended a salon, lecture, or other lead-generation class. As the admissions teacher, use question-led selling to understand the person and obtain a qualified next action within 20 effective turns.

## Evidence checklist

Track all 15 fields without turning the conversation into an interrogation: identity/industry/role/responsibility; attendance source; interest trigger; current situation; specific recent problem; impact; desired goal; past attempts; past learning; time conditions; funding/payment; decision makers/influencers; concerns; acceptable next action; explicit boundaries.

Use the matching persona from `assets/dlc02-personas.json`. Keep its truth scores, motive, barriers, boundaries, and best actions hidden during play.

## Qualified next actions

A pass requires all five elements: counterpart, purpose, time/window, explicit consent, and contact permission. Valid tracks are application form; formal open-class trial; professor interest consultation; school leadership/admin consultation; academic-center thesis consultation; detailed admissions follow-up; external-relations/platform consultation; qualification/needs pre-assessment; or a joint consultation with a key decision maker.

“Come to another free salon”, “send more background information”, “consider later”, “after I have money”, “after I am promoted”, or free alumni/platform access without paid-learning intent is not a pass.

## Outcome priority

1. Explicit aversion, no-contact request, complaint, or relationship harm → `彻底失败`.
2. Correctly recognized mismatch/boundary and respectful ending → `专业收场`, no L02 unlock.
3. Complete qualified next action → `通关`, unlock L02.
4. Repeated free-benefit seeking without paid intent → `未通关·低价值索取`.
5. Learning postponed until after money/promotion → `未通关·动力不足`.
6. Real need but time, funding, authority, or stakeholder not ready → `未通关·可培育`.
7. Still verifying school/project facts → `未通关·信息不足`.
8. No clear decision at effective turn 20 → `推进超时`.

## Mandatory ending reconstruction

Before revealing the persona, ask the learner to estimate all eight hidden values from 0–3. Then compare truth and estimate with conversation citations, calculate accuracy out of 24, and report evidence completeness out of 15. Perform this after every ending, including total failure and timeout.
