# Privacy and boundaries

Before training, state whether microphone input is enabled, whether any online recognition/TTS is allowed, what transcript is saved, and the local log path.

Default to fictional customers and synthetic business details. Do not enter real personal information, confidential pricing, trade secrets, credentials, medical details, financial account data, or regulated records.

A clear refusal ends sales advancement. The learner may thank the customer and ask whether future contact is permitted, but silence or ambiguity is not permission. A mismatch, delayed decision, refusal, or referral is a valid customer outcome.

For group classrooms, default every learner to text input to avoid microphone interference. Voice and text may be switched within one session without changing the scoring contract.
