# Teaching feedback and full review

## Default per-turn output

Every teaching-mode turn contains both layers:

```text
【客户】<context-specific customer response>

（教练分析：
- 本轮判断：<observable behavior classification>
- 做得好的地方：<one evidence-based strength>
- 可以改进的地方：<one concrete issue and why it matters>
- 更好的表达示例：<a usable rewritten sentence>
- 当前关卡进度：<stage and discovered evidence; no hidden facts>
）
```

The coach analysis follows the customer response; it never replaces it. Do not expose private D/T/O values, scripted hidden motives, or the branch answer the learner has not earned. Give enough guidance to prevent blind guessing.

## Immersive mode

When explicitly requested, suppress the per-turn block but continue recording evidence. Show coaching when the learner pauses, reaches a v0.3 checkpoint, requests help, or finishes. Resume the same customer state after coaching.

## Full review

Retain the complete v0.3 review structure:

- stage, persona, outcome, and turn count;
- scored dimensions with cited turns;
- at least two strengths and two improvements;
- rewritten examples;
- red lines and prompt counts;
- theory/source mapping;
- retry, difficulty, persona, and next-stage recommendations.

Add the v0.4 eight-dimension 0–24 capability score and independent customer outcome; do not delete the v0.3 evidence, theory citations, or examples.
